Our Story

Founded on the belief that
the material comes first.

Monsieur is the digital house of Aelier Groupe - a tailoring atelier built on exceptional natural fibres, expert craftsmanship, and a deeply personal client relationship.

Every piece begins with the cloth: the rarest cashmere, tropical wools, Irish linens and Sea Island cottons, each selected for character and longevity. From there, it is shaped by hand in Italy, finished with patience, and made to be worn for years rather than seasons.
