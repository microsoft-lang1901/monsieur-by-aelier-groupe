# MONSIEUR Manual QA Script

Run this against the phone preview or native build.

1. Open Home and confirm the editorial hero, lead copy, and featured products render.
2. Open Collections and confirm all generated collection chapters show catalogue products.
3. Open Search and test material search, category filters, collection filters, clear filters, and product result navigation.
4. Open a Product Detail page and confirm hero image, identity, Fibre Passport, construction, colour, styling notes, size guide, save action, and checkout action.
5. Save a product, open Wardrobe, and confirm saved pieces plus related recommendations.
6. Open Checkout from a product and complete the test confirmation.
7. Open Account and confirm order archive, notification persistence, and private consultation request recording.
8. Rotate text size/accessibility settings and confirm content remains readable and controls remain usable.
9. Confirm no blocked commerce language or payment methods appear anywhere.
